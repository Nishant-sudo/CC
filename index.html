Courses We Offer

We offer three top computer science courses for students at Fateh Group of College

BCA

The BCA course is a full time three-year (six semesters) Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Applications. The degree helps students set up a strong academic base for an advanced career in Computer Applications.

BSC-IT

BSc (Bachelor of Science) in IT is about storing, processing, securing, and managing information. This degree focuses on subjects such as software, databases, and networking.

BSC CA & IT

The Bachelor of Science in (CS & IT) is an undergraduate degree course in computer applications for a duration of 3 years. The field relates to the design, development, and use of computer applications.
